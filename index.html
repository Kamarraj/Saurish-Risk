Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Saurish Risk Platform.

What is the difference between Free Basic and Pro?

The Free plan lets you calculate position sizes with a $250 max trade risk cap. The Pro plan removes all risk caps, unlocks Prop Firm rule auto-sync (FTMO, Apex, etc.), and includes advanced loss-prevention guards.

Does it support Futures and Micro Contracts?

Yes! Pre-configured settings are built-in for Micro Nasdaq (MNQ), Micro S&P (MES), Gold (MGC), Crude Oil (MCL), Forex pairs, and Cryptocurrencies.

How does it prevent daily drawdown breaches?

It continuously calculates your active account equity against maximum permitted daily loss percentages and triggers visual alerts before you take an oversized trade.
